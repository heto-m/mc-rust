## Decisions

- **Per-renderer pose stacks.** `Plan::unflipped(scale)` drops both the `(-1,-1,1)` flip and the living model drop for renderers that draw their model without them (llama spit, trident, wind charge, end crystal); renderers that keep the flip but not the drop (leash knot, wither skull, shulker bullet) set `model_y = 0`. `Plan.pre_offset` is a translation applied before the yaw/pitch rotations, like vanilla's `translate` before `rotateDegrees`.
- **Audit result.** Wrong (all floated 1.5 blocks): leash knot, llama spit, shulker bullet, wind charge, breeze wind charge, trident, wither skull. Right: end crystal (its 2.002 offset cancelled the drop; now written as vanilla's -1), evoker fangs (a living-style stack with the drop), dragon fireball, experience orb, bobber, thrown items and experience bottle, eye of ender, falling block (free geometry in entity space), minecarts and boats (round 3). Firework had a leftover 0.125 lift. Item frames, paintings, display entities and item entities are free geometry, not model parts, so they never had the drop.
- **Shulker bullet.** Two passes of the same model: opaque, and a 1.5x shell with tint alpha 38 (`654311423`). Block light is 15 (`Plan.bright` now applies to model parts too). The model's `main` bone takes yaw and pitch like `ShulkerBulletModel.setupAnim`.
- **Wind charge.** `wind` and `wind_charge` bones counter-rotate at 16 degrees per tick; the texture scrolls by `age * 0.03` in u.
- **Creeper swell.** The synched index 16 is the swell direction, not progress, so the client runs `Creeper.tick`'s counter (`swell`, `oldSwell`, ignite flag forces direction 1, clamp 0..30) and exposes `getSwelling(partial)` as `EntityView.swelling`. The white overlay reuses the entity shader's fixed 75% white flag, switched on for `(int)(swelling * 10) % 2 != 0` through `Plan.flash_parts`; vanilla's strength varies with `clamp(swelling, 0.5, 1)`, an accepted approximation.
- **Dark cutout mips.** Base level: fully transparent texels take 3/4 of the darkest opaque colour. Each mip: sum of the linear channels (alpha included) of the non-empty texels divided by four, then the existing coverage rescale with bias.
- **Not done.** Breeze wind uses the translucent pipeline with a scrolled UV rather than vanilla's dedicated render type; trident glint, the ominous item spawner, the cushion entity, display billboard modes and the dragon's pitch lean remain.
